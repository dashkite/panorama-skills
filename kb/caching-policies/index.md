# Caching Policies

This guide outlines performance caching rules, freshness criteria, and invalidation conventions evaluated by Enchant.

## Performance Optimization
Caching policies ensure that data is stored close to the client/origin and invalidated only when specifications or source resources change.
- **Freshness Evaluation**: Automated time-to-live (TTL) and dependency-based cache evaluation.
- **Edge Caches**: Edge-parity distribution of cache stores.
